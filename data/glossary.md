# Glossary

## genre: pop
Pop is polished, hook-driven music with catchy choruses and bright production. It is usually danceable and upbeat.

## genre: indie pop
Indie pop is pop with a lighter, homemade feel: jangly or synthy textures, warm vocals and cheerful melodies.

## genre: lofi
Lofi is mellow, low-tempo instrumental music with vinyl crackle and soft beats. It is popular as background music for studying, coding and relaxing.

## genre: ambient
Ambient music is slow, atmospheric and mostly beatless. It favors texture over melody and suits focus, sleep and meditation.

## genre: jazz
Jazz features improvisation, acoustic instruments and swung rhythms. Mellow jazz fits cafes and quiet evenings.

## genre: rock
Rock is guitar-driven music with strong drums and high energy, often loud and driving.

## genre: metal
Metal is a heavier, faster and more aggressive branch of rock, with distorted guitars and intense drumming.

## genre: synthwave
Synthwave is retro 1980s-inspired electronic music with analog synth lines and steady pulsing beats, often linked to night drives.

## genre: country
Country is story-driven music with acoustic guitar and a nostalgic, down-to-earth feel.

## genre: hip hop
Hip hop is rhythm and beat focused, with a strong bassline and rapped vocals. It often sounds confident and danceable.

## genre: r&b
R&B blends smooth vocals with soulful grooves. It is warm, sensual and mid-tempo.

## genre: classical
Classical music is orchestral or piano-based, acoustic and often slow and emotional.

## genre: latin
Latin music covers rhythmic styles such as salsa, with percussion, brass and very danceable, joyful energy.

## genre: folk
Folk is acoustic, gentle and storytelling-oriented, with guitar and soft vocals.

## genre: techno
Techno is repetitive, hypnotic electronic dance music with a driving four-on-the-floor beat, made for clubs.

## genre: reggae
Reggae has an off-beat guitar rhythm, a relaxed groove and a sunny, laid-back mood.

## genre: shoegaze
Shoegaze layers washes of distorted, reverberant guitar into a dreamy wall of sound with buried vocals.

## mood: happy
A happy song has a high valence: bright, cheerful and uplifting.

## mood: chill
A chill song is low energy and relaxed. It is good background music for studying or unwinding.

## mood: focused
A focused song is steady and unobtrusive, with little change, which helps concentration.

## mood: relaxed
A relaxed song is slow and warm, calming without being sleepy.

## mood: intense
An intense song is high energy and powerful, suited to workouts and adrenaline.

## mood: moody
A moody song is atmospheric and a little dark, with mid energy and low valence.

## mood: nostalgic
A nostalgic song evokes memories, usually with warm acoustic sounds and mid tempo.

## mood: confident
A confident song is bold and swaggering with a strong beat.

## mood: romantic
A romantic song is smooth, warm and intimate.

## mood: angry
An angry song is very high energy with low valence: aggressive, fast and loud.

## mood: melancholy
A melancholy song is slow, quiet and sad, with low valence and low energy.

## mood: energetic
An energetic song is fast, lively and makes you want to dance.

## mood: peaceful
A peaceful song is quiet, gentle and acoustic, ideal for calm moments.

## mood: euphoric
A euphoric song builds to an ecstatic, high-energy peak, typical of dance music.

## mood: laidback
A laidback song is easygoing and sunny, with a relaxed pace.

## mood: dreamy
A dreamy song is hazy and floating, with soft textures and a sense of drift.

## audio features
Energy measures intensity from 0 (calm) to 1 (intense). Valence measures musical positivity from 0 (sad) to 1 (happy). Danceability measures how suited a song is for dancing. Acousticness measures how acoustic rather than electronic it is. Tempo is in beats per minute (BPM).
